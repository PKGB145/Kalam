# Kalam ✨

### Your calm digital study desk

Kalam is a cozy all-in-one study space built using pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — designed for students who want a clean, distraction-free place to learn, focus, organize, and think.

Instead of feeling like a complicated productivity app, Kalam feels more like:

* a notebook,
* a study desk,
* and a personal workspace made just for you.

---

# 🌟 What You Can Do

## 📊 Stay organized

Track your:

* habits
* tasks
* goals
* assignments
* study sessions
* daily progress

All from one dashboard.

---

## 📝 Write freely

Kalam includes a built-in journal and notes system where you can:

* brainstorm ideas
* write reflections
* keep study notes
* save quick thoughts
* organize subjects

---

## 📚 Build your study space

The Study Space includes:

* notebook-style notes
* flashcards
* quiz mode
* mind maps
* source/document management
* JSON export/import

It’s designed to feel like your own digital notebook.

## 🍅 Focus better

Use the Pomodoro timer to:

* stay focused

* avoid burnout

Includes:

* short breaks

# 💾 Your Data Stays Yours

Kalam works fully locally using:

```txt id="8r0z5r"
localStorage
```

This means:

* no accounts
* no tracking
* no ads
* no servers

Your data stays on your device unless you export it yourself.
